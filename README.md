# The Dissertation Rescue Toolkit

Dissertation Rescue Plan Free Tools

A free Excel workbook that turns the tools in *The Dissertation Rescue Plan* (Mitch Stimers, PhD) into working spreadsheets. Each tab matches a chapter of the book. You type or choose your entries, and the formulas track progress, flag overdue follow-ups, and spot alignment gaps for you.

## Download

1. Click **Dissertation-Rescue-Toolkit.xlsx** in the file list above.
2. Click the **Download raw file** button (the downward arrow at the top right of the file view).
3. Open the file in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc.
4. Save a copy under a new name before you start, so you always keep a clean version.

To use the workbook in Google Sheets, upload the file to Google Drive and choose **Open with > Google Sheets**.

## What's Inside

| Tab | What it does | Book chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Start Here | Instructions, color key, and links to every tab | |
| Feedback Tracker | Logs each reviewer comment, your revision, your response, and its status; counts open items and flags major comments still open | Chapter 5 |
| Feedback Decoder | Translates common committee comments into action | Chapter 5 |
| Writing Log | Records each writing session; totals minutes per week, counts days written, tracks your streak, and charts the last 12 weeks | Chapters 3 and 8 |
| Follow-Up Log | Tracks emails awaiting replies, flags a follow-up at 7 days and an overdue item at 10 days, and suggests the next rung of the escalation ladder | Chapters 5 and 9 |
| Chapter Progress | Compares your page counts with typical ranges for each chapter and tracks each chapter's draft status | Chapter 4 |
| Alignment Matrix | Maps each research question to its data, analysis, framework, and Chapter 4 and 5 sections; lists what's missing; includes the five-item self-check | Chapter 7 |
| Weekly Planner | Pick a Mini, Medium, or Max week and plan sprints against that target; includes the writing menu and writing identities | Chapter 8 |
| Formatting Checklist | The final submission checklist and the 10 common formatting mistakes, with a percent-complete bar | Chapter 6 |

## How to Use It

- **Yellow cells** are for your entries. Many offer a dropdown list.
- **Gray cells** hold formulas. Leave them alone, and they update on their own.
- **Gray italic rows** are examples. Overwrite each one with your first real entry.
- The typical page ranges in Chapter Progress and the weekly targets in the Weekly Planner are editable. Replace them with your program's numbers.

## Compatibility

The workbook runs in Excel 2019, Microsoft 365, Excel for Mac, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice Calc. Excel 2016 and earlier lack the TEXTJOIN function, so the "Missing items" column in the Alignment Matrix shows an error there; every other tab works.

## Privacy

The workbook contains no macros and no external connections. Everything you enter stays in your copy of the file.
